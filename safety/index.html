My Bubble isn't for emergencies — call 911.

Report it in the app

You can report a message or a person.

On a teen's phone, the control says "report". You can also choose "report and block".

The owner can report from the bubble they are reading. The owner does not block from that screen. The owner can take a message out ("take out") or take a person out of one bubble.

You will be asked what is wrong:

  • bullying or threats
  • offensive or gross
  • sexual content involving someone under 18
  • something else

You can add a short note. It goes to the people who run My Bubble. It does not go to the person you reported.

What we do next

We read every report.

We keep a private copy of what you reported. Reported content may be kept as long as needed for safety and legal reasons. We report illegal content to the authorities as required by law.

Taking a message out, blocking someone, or deleting an account does not erase that private copy. The copy is not shown in the chat.

A word filter refuses some words in messages, names, and bubble names. It will not catch everything. Reporting is how a person reaches us.

Block

A teen can block another teen. The control says "block them".

If you block someone, you both leave every bubble you share. The people who set up both phones are told that a block happened. They are not sent the message, and they are not told the other teen's name on the lock screen.

A block is for when something is really wrong. Taking someone out of one bubble is separate. That does not block them everywhere.

A block is not undone by a scan. Two people who are blocked cannot join a bubble together.
