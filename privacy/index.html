My Bubble is a messaging app for people 13 and older. It is run by Digital & Disruptive LLC, d.b.a. My Bubble, an Arizona limited liability company, at 5227 North 7th Street, Phoenix, AZ 85014.

The owner creates the account with an email and a password, then sets up each teen's phone with a code. The owner is responsible for the account. Teens do not have an email or a password.

Who it is for

The app is for ages 13 and up. The app isn't for anyone under 13.

At sign-up the owner confirms they are 18 or older and that each teen they add is 13 or older. We do not collect birthdates.

If we learn a user is under 13, we take them off the account and delete their information.

We follow applicable law.

What we collect

From the owner:

  • An email and a password, used only to sign in. The password is not kept in our own tables.
  • A household name, such as the label other families see.
  • A push token, if notifications are on.

From a teen, on that phone:

  • A display name they type.
  • A face made of drawn parts: shape, mouth, color, and extras. This is a recipe, not a face scan.
  • Messages they send.
  • Reactions they add. One reaction per person per message, from a fixed set.
  • Bubble names.
  • Which bubbles they are in, and when they joined or left.

On the phone:

  • An anonymous session for that device. The teen never types a password.
  • A device check, so we can tell the session belongs to that phone. We store a device key. We do not copy the phone.
  • A push token, so we can send a notification. A notification does not include the message text. An owner's notification does not name the other teen or the other family.

If someone reports a message or a person, we keep a private copy: what was reported, who reported it, and a short note if they wrote one. That copy is not shown in the app.

If an owner asks another owner for a way to reach them, we do not already have a contact list. The other owner types what they want to share, for that one ask. We do not hand out the owner's email on our own.

We also keep short security records: one-time codes for setting up a phone or joining a bubble, rate-limit counters, and a note when a phone fails a device check. That note says which check failed. It does not include a message.

Who can see it

The only way to connect is to scan a code on someone else's phone, in person. There is no username, no search, no phone number, and no invite link.

The owner can read the bubbles their teen is in, including members and what was shared. The owner cannot post. Teens are told this. Both sides are told again when a scan connects two people.

Other people in a bubble see a teen's display name and drawn face. They do not see the owner's email.

When a teen leaves a bubble, or is blocked, that teen's owner can still read what happened up to that moment, and nothing after.

One family's account is not readable by another family's owner.

What we don't do

We don't sell or share your information, and we don't track you across other apps.

  • We do not show ads.
  • We do not collect location, contacts, browsing history, or an advertising ID.
  • We do not let anyone find a person by name, phone number, or email.
  • We do not let a teen post in a bubble the owner cannot read.
  • We do not encrypt messages so that we cannot read them. The owner can read them. That is the product.

Your rights

You can see, correct, and delete your information.

  • See it: save a copy in the app.
  • Correct it in the app.
  • Delete it in the app. The owner deletes the account, or removes a teen.

We don't export or delete accounts by email.

Where it is stored

Data is stored in the United States with Supabase (on Amazon Web Services, US West). The app talks to that service over HTTPS.

Service providers:

  • Supabase, on Amazon Web Services (US West), stores the data.
  • Apple delivers notifications and checks that the app is genuine.

We do not use analytics or advertising providers.

How long we keep it

A message is marked to be cleared one year after it was sent. A popped bubble is marked to be cleared one year after it popped. The message text goes. The fact that the bubble existed can remain, so other families' chats still make sense.

Reported content may be kept as long as needed for safety and legal reasons. We report illegal content to the authorities as required by law.

Push leftovers are kept for about 7 days. One-time codes and rate-limit rows are deleted when they expire. A contact an owner chose to share is marked to be cleared after one year.

Notes from failed device checks are kept 90 days, then deleted.

Delete and save a copy

The owner can delete the account in the app. The owner can also remove one teen without closing the account. A teen cannot do either alone. They ask the owner.

Sign-in stops right away.

Your information is deleted within 30 days. In bubbles you were in, others see that a message was taken out, but not what it said or who sent it.

Some reported content may be kept as required by law. That copy is not shown in the app.

One family leaving does not delete a bubble that other families are still in.

The owner can save a copy in the app and share it from the phone. We do not email the file. The copy includes the owner's profile, each teen's profile, the messages that teen sent, including ones taken out after a report, memberships, devices, and reports that teen or that owner filed. It does not include other families' message text, emails, or household labels. Other teens appear as the name and face already shown in that bubble.

Save a copy and delete in the app. We don't export or delete accounts by email.
