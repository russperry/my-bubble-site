The deal

My Bubble is for people 13 and older. If you are under 13, do not use My Bubble.

The owner creates the account and sets up each teen's phone. At sign-up the owner confirms they are 18 or older, and that each teen they add is 13 or older. The owner is responsible for the account.

If you use the app, these terms and the privacy policy apply.

If we learn a user is under 13, we take them off the account and delete their information.

The account

The owner holds the account. Sign-in is the owner's email and password. That is the only way back in.

A teen has no email and no password. The owner sets up the teen's phone with a code, in person. The session stays on that phone.

A teen cannot delete the account, take themselves off it, or transfer it. They ask the owner. The owner does it in the app.

We do not hand the account to the teen at 13 or at 18. When a teen turns 18, the owner can take them off, and the teen can start their own account.

How people connect

The only way to connect is to scan a code on someone else's phone, in person. There is no username, no search, no friend request, and no invite to text.

Do not go around the scan. We may close an account that does.

What you can send

Text, a display name, a bubble name, a drawn face, and a reaction. A teen cannot edit or unsend a message.

The owner can read their teen's bubbles. The owner cannot post. The owner can take a message out. The chat shows that it was taken out.

The owner can take a teen out of a bubble. A teen can block another teen. A block takes both of them out of every bubble they share.

A word filter refuses some words in messages, names, and bubble names. It will not catch everything. Do not send anything illegal. Do not send sexual content involving anyone under 18.

Reports

Anyone in a bubble can report a message or a person. The safety page says how.

We report illegal content to the authorities as required by law. We keep a private copy, as the privacy policy describes.

Deleting

The owner can delete the account in the app, or remove one teen. A teen cannot do this alone.

Your information is deleted within 30 days. In bubbles you were in, others see that a message was taken out, but not what it said or who sent it.

Some reported content may be kept as required by law. Closing an account does not erase a bubble other families are still in.
