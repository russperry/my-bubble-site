safety and grown-ups

how safety works

  • a parent or guardian sets it up, and chooses when you can add new people.

  • they can read the bubbles you're in, and everyone in the bubble knows that.

  • every message and every person has a report button. a person reads every report.

  • blocking takes you both out of the bubbles you share.
